DIGITAL WELLBEING

SECURITY FRAMEWORK (DWSF)

Building digital wellbeing programs that last.

A step-by-step framework for establishing, assessing, strengthening, and sustaining digital wellbeing in schools to help prepare the next generation for the challenges of the internet.

Nearly 3 in 4 young people have encountered harmful content online 1. As schools worldwide rethink their digital safeguards and governments introduce social media restrictions, society is taking decisive action against these digital risks.

With clear risk visibility, prioritised resource allocation, and a proven duty-of-care framework, DWSF equips children, teachers, and parents with the tools and knowledge to navigate the digital world safely.

Our project is part of a growing shift from reactive crisis management to proactive digital resilience — helping schools move ahead of harm, not just respond to it. Together, we can build a safer online environment and foster responsible digital citizenship.

We are preparing to trial the assessment with schools soon. If you'd like to learn more or get involved, please reach out.

The DWSF Solution: 5-Pillar Benchmarking Framework

DWSF is based on the 4Cs of online harm, a framework that groups all forms of online risk into four categories; Content, Contact, Conduct, Contract.2.

We use this internationally recognised classification as the core of our how we evaluate a school's digital safety maturity. We then embed each with our experience of industry security best-practice and behavioural science research and wrap them all with a governance layer to ensure visibility, accountability and continuous improvement.

1. Educated Child

Assesses student digital wellbeing awareness training; literacy, agency, clear reporting channels, and peer support systems. Ensures students can identify risks and safely ask for help for themselves and their peers.

2. Engaged Parent

Evaluates community outreach; family workshops, and transparent communication regarding home-to-school device rules and digital citizenship.

3. Insightful Educator

Ensures Educators are equipped with the knowledge & resources; to be up-to-date with harmful online trends, identifying, responding & supporting potentially harmful digital activity.

4. Accessible Help

Evaluates easily reachable & trusted reporting tools, student support pathways, clear staff escalation protocols, incident response readiness, anonymous reporting mechanisms, and immediate crisis support.

5. Governance

Provides overarching executive leadership, policy frameworks, staff compliance training, and continuous legal duty-of-care alignment across all pillars.

About Us

Philip Sandamas / Founder

Security Professional & Online Safety Advocate

Philip is a security professional with over two decades helping government departments and organisations enhance their physical and cyber protections.

As a parent, Philip noticed other parents grow increasingly concerned about how much time their children spend online.

That concern sparked an idea: combine cybersecurity industry best-practice with psychology principles and apply them to online safety.

The result is a framework that helps schools build a Digital Wellbeing Program from scratch — or assess and strengthen an existing one — so protective measures can adapt and improve over time and always do the very best to enable our society to grow and be prosperous.
